What runs behind the chat

For your developers. One small service sits between your Shopify store, WhatsApp and a payment link. Your store stays the source of truth for orders and customers.

  • Shopify webhooks and Admin API
  • WhatsApp Cloud API
  • Razorpay payment links for UPI
  • Python (FastAPI) or Node
  • Postgres
  • Runs on the cloud your team already uses

    Message templates
    
    
    WhatsApp only lets a business start a conversation with a template Meta has approved. These three cover the whole flow. Highlighted parts are filled in per customer.
    
    
    
      
    
        
    delivery_optinUtilityEnglish
    
        
    Hi Riya, your Ratnagiri Peaberry was delivered today. Want a message before this bag runs out? We time it to our roast days.
    Yes pleaseNo thanks
    
        
    Sent when Shopify marks the order delivered. A yes is the customer's opt-in.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    reorder_reminderMarketingEnglish
    
        
    Hi Riya, your Ratnagiri runs out around Sun 1 Nov. Our next roast is Wed 28 Oct. Reorder by 6 pm Tue 27 Oct and it reaches you around Sat 31 Oct.
    Reorder sameTry something newPause for 2 weeks
    
        
    Sent at 9 am IST, two days before the target roast.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    cafe_weekly_checkUtilityEnglish
    
        
    Good morning, Third Wave Corner. Your usual 5 kg of LEAN Blend for Wednesday's roast?
    Yes, send itChange quantitySkip this week
    
        
    Sent at 8:30 am two days before each roast to cafes with a standing order.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    Meta is likely to class a reminder that suggests buying as Marketing, which costs more per message than Utility. We submit it that way so it doesn't get rejected later. Replies inside 24 hours are free-form and need no template.
    
  
    

    


    
  
    
    
    The timing rules
    
    
    This is the part that makes a reminder useful instead of annoying. Every number below is a setting your team can change.
    
    
    
      
    
        # Riya: 250 g bag, V60, 2 cups a day
    
        grams_a_day = 15 g × 2 cups = 30 g
    
        run_out     = Sat 24 Oct + floor(250 ÷ 30) days = Sun 1 Nov
    
        roast       = latest Wednesday where roast + 3 days ≤ run_out − 1 = Wed 28 Oct
    
        remind_at   = roast − 2 days, 09:00 IST = Mon 26 Oct, 9:00 am
      
    
      
      
        
    1. 1Grams a cup come from the brew methodV60 15 g, AeroPress 15 g, French press 16 g, espresso 18 g, South Indian filter 10 g.
      2. 
        
    2. 2Orders close at 6 pm the day before a roastA reply after that goes into the next roast, and the customer is told the new date.
      3. 
        
    3. 3If no roast can make it in timeThe reminder goes for the first possible roast and suggests a bigger bag. At 3 cups a day, 250 g lasts 5 days.
      4. 
        
    4. 4One follow-up, then quietNo reply in 24 hours gets one gentle follow-up. After that, nothing until the next bag.
      5. 
      
    
      
      
        
    1. 5No double messagesCustomers already on a website subscription are skipped. Each customer has at most one open reminder.
      2. 
        
    2. 6Cafes keep their usual order by defaultNo reply by 6 pm keeps the standing order. SKIP leaves a week out.
      3. 
        
    3. 7Quiet hoursNothing goes out before 9 am or after 8 pm IST.
      4. 
        
    4. 8STOP works any timeOne word ends all messages for that number, and the opt-out is recorded.

    What stays yours
    
    
    Nothing here replaces the tools your team already runs.
    
    
    
      
    Orders stay in Shopify
    Every reorder is created as a normal paid Shopify order with a whatsapp-reorder tag, so fulfilment, invoices and reports work as they do today.
    
      
    The service keeps very little
    Phone number, coffee, bag size, cups a day and reminder dates. No card or UPI details ever touch it; Razorpay handles payment.
    
      
    Your team can take it over
    It runs on your cloud account, under your WhatsApp Business number, with the code handed over and documented.
